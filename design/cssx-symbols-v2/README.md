# CSSX symbol exploration · second visual direction

The supplied symbol anthology is the visual reference. It replaces the first exploration's soft outlined curves with compact abstract emblems: solid mass, geometric repetition, deliberate counterforms, and strong monochrome silhouettes.

The reference is used for its graphic language, not as a selection of symbols to reproduce. Proposed marks are original explorations. The source image is preserved as `reference.png`.

## Identity premise

CSSX gives styling decisions a composable structure and resolves static authoring into compact output. The proposed identity centers on **clarity through composition**, expressed with a deliberate, lucid, assured personality.

Useful visual principles:

- A small number of forms governed by one repeatable construction rule.
- Positive mass and negative space carry equal importance.
- Compact silhouettes that can stand beside the CSSX name without needing animation.
- A visible relationship to X, composition, or consolidation; avoid forcing all four letters into one mark.
- Monochrome first. Color, wordmark refinement, and motion follow a selected direction.

## Exploration families

1. **Weave** — interlocking bands that compose into a coherent X.
2. **Fold** — an architectural enclosure surrounding a solid X.
3. **Strata** — repeated strokes consolidating into a compact geometric system.
4. **Counterform** — solid curved modules define an X through the space between them.

These are concept images generated with the built-in image-generation tool. They are not production SVG artwork or approved replacements for CSSX's current identity. Geometric reconstruction and optical correction should follow selection.

## Review

**Strata is the recommended direction to develop:** repetition and negative space directly express composition, and the construction feels closest to the supplied reference. Weave is a stronger, more assertive alternative. Fold is more literal and badge-like. Counterform has the simplest silhouette but offers less distinction on its own.

All four images were visually inspected. Strata uses two chevron bands per side rather than the three initially requested in its prompt; the simpler result was retained. Fold uses a solid X rather than the requested negative-space X. A refinement introduced unwanted outlines, so the cleaner original was retained. Counterform has small edge irregularities that require correction in a future vector drawing.

Each PNG has an adjacent `-prompt.txt` file recording its generation instructions. The prompts describe intended construction; the images and these review notes describe the actual results.
